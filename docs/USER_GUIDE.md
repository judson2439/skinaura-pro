## Overview

**SkinAura PRO** helps skincare professionals and their clients stay connected between visits:

- **Professionals** create routines, assign them to clients, track progress photos, run virtual consults, and keep everything in one place (treatment plans, product library, analytics).
- **Clients** follow their assigned routine, log progress photos, view treatment plans and documents, earn achievements, and message their provider.

You sign in once; the app takes you to your **Dashboard** (professional or client) depending on your account type.

# Professional workflow

This section is for **skincare professionals** (estheticians, providers) using SkinAura PRO.

---

## Getting started as a professional

1. **Sign in** with your professional account. You’ll land on your **Dashboard**.
2. On first sign-in you may see a **welcome video** that introduces SkinAura PRO. You can close it when you’re ready.
3. Use the **left sidebar** to move between sections. Your **profile** and **Active Clients** count appear at the bottom of the sidebar.

## Dashboard

Your **Dashboard** is the main overview:

- **Stats**: Total clients, how many completed their routine today, how many need attention, and average compliance.
- **Client list**: All your clients with key info (name, streak, level, whether they completed today, compliance).
- **Actions**: Open a client’s **profile**, or send them an **SMS nudge** (reminder or check-in).

Use the dashboard to see who’s on track and who might need a nudge or a message.

## My Clients

Here you manage everyone you’re working with.

- **Client list**: Search and see clients with their streak, level, compliance, and whether they completed their routine today.
- **Add client**: Use **Invite by email** to send an invitation. The client receives a link; when they sign up and accept, they’re linked to you.
- **Open a client**: Click a client to open their **profile** (notes, routines, treatment plans, progress photos, chat).
- **Send SMS**: Send a short nudge or reminder (e.g. “Don’t forget your PM routine”) to a client’s phone.

If you see “Already invited” for an email, that address was already sent an invite; wait for them to accept or check their spam folder.

---

## Client Photos

**Client Photos** shows progress photos uploaded by your clients.

- You’ll only see photos once you have **linked clients** (add them from **My Clients** first).
- Open a photo to **view it**, add **comments**, and use **annotation/markup** tools to highlight areas or give feedback.
- Your feedback is visible to the client so they know what’s improving and what to watch.

Use this section on your chosen review cadence (e.g. weekly or biweekly) to keep routines aligned with how skin is actually changing.

---

## Facial Scan

**Facial Scan** (Facial Scan reports) shows **client facial scan / face-age results** when clients use the Face Analysis feature on their side.

- View reports per client.
- Mark reports as **reviewed** (e.g. “checked”) so you can track what you’ve already seen.
- Use this to inform routine or product adjustments and follow-ups.

---

## Manage Routines

Here you **create and assign** routines.

- **Create a routine**: Name it (e.g. “AM – Acne”), choose schedule type (morning, evening, weekly, custom), add **steps** (cleanse, treat, hydrate, protect). For each step you can add notes, product recommendations, and link products from your **Product Library**.
- **Use templates**: You can apply **platform templates** (if your admin has set them) to create a new routine quickly, then edit steps and products.
- **Assign to clients**: Once a routine is saved, assign it to one or more clients. They’ll see it under **My Routine** on their app.
- **Edit or delete**: You can edit an existing routine (steps, products, notes) or remove it. If it’s already assigned, consider how changes affect current clients.

Best practice: build routines in a clear order (e.g. cleanse → treat → hydrate → protect for AM) and add short “Week 1 expectations” and “What to stop if irritation occurs” so clients know what to do.

---

## Treatment Plans

**Treatment Plans** are high-level plans for a client (goals, timeline, milestones, appointments, documents).

- **Create a plan**: Choose a client, add title, description, goals, start/end date, and notes.
- **Add milestones**: Mark important checkpoints (e.g. “Week 4 check-in,” “Barrier repair complete”). You or the client can mark them complete.
- **Add products and routines**: Attach recommended products and assign routines to the plan so everything stays in one place.
- **Appointments**: Add follow-up or consult dates; you can update status (e.g. completed) later.
- **PDFs**: Upload handouts, care guides, or diagrams. Clients see these in their **Treatment Plans** section and can open or download them.

Everything in a treatment plan is visible to that client in their app under **Treatment Plans**.

---

## Analytics

**Analytics** gives you charts and summaries (e.g. compliance over time, routine completions, client activity). Use it to see trends and which clients are most engaged so you can adjust support or outreach.

---

## Product Library

Your **Product Library** is the set of products you recommend and link to routines.

- **Add products**: Enter name, brand, category, description, usage, and optionally price and purchase link. You can upload a product image.
- **Bulk import**: Add many products at once (e.g. from a CSV or Shopify if connected).
- **Link to steps**: In **Manage Routines**, when editing a routine step, you can link one or more products from the library so clients see exactly what to use.
- **Shopify** (if enabled): Connect your Shopify store to pull in products or keep them in sync.

Clients do **not** order through SkinAura PRO; they see recommended products and purchase elsewhere (e.g. your store or retail) unless you give them a link.

---

## Virtual Consult

**Virtual Consult** explains how consults fit into your workflow:

- **Scan → Plan → Track → Adjust**: Client fills an intake form (e.g. via link); you review it, run a live consult (e.g. via SkinAura PRO Circle Space / Live Room), then update their profile (session summary, routine, treatment plan, upload PDFs). You then **track** progress (photos, compliance) and **adjust** (SMS, routine changes, follow-up consults).
- **Consult Room**: The **Open My Consult Room** button takes you to the external consult/video platform (e.g. Circle) where you schedule or join Live Room events. You share the event link with the client so they can join.
- **After the consult**: All updates (summary, routines, treatment plan, PDFs) live in that client’s SkinAura PRO profile. Clients see treatment plans and PDFs in their app.

The page also includes **FAQs** (e.g. how the client joins, where intake forms go, why the consult opens in a new tab, recording, and SMS nudges).

## Upload Logo & PDF Upload

- **Upload Logo**: Add your practice or brand logo so it can be used where the app shows your branding.
- **PDF Upload**: Upload documents (e.g. care guides, handouts) that you can then attach to **treatment plans** or use for clients. Clients access these from their **Treatment Plans** section.

**Notifications** shows in-app alerts (e.g. new client accepted invite, new message, new progress photo). Use the bell icon in the header to see unread count. Open **Notifications** from the sidebar to read and clear them.

# Client workflow

This section is for **clients** (people working with a skincare professional) using SkinAura PRO.

---

## Getting started as a client

You can join SkinAura PRO in two ways:

1. **Invitation from your provider**
  Your professional sends an **invite by email**. You receive a link to sign up. After you create your account and verify your email, you can **accept** the invitation. You may be asked to **complete your profile** (name, skin type, concerns) on a dedicated page. Once connected, you’ll see your provider’s routines and plans.
2. **Signing up on your own**
  You can also sign up from the main site. If your provider has already invited you, accept the invitation from **Notifications** (see below) so you’re linked and can see your routine.

After you sign in, you’ll land on your **Dashboard**. On **first login** you may see:

- A **welcome video** introducing SkinAura PRO  
- A **welcome / guide modal** with a short overview

You can close these and explore the app. The **Guide** section in the sidebar has the same getting-started info anytime.

---

## Dashboard

Your **Dashboard** is your home screen:

- **Your stats**: Level (e.g. Bronze, Silver, Gold), points, and **current streak** (consecutive days you’ve completed a routine).
- **Routines**: A summary of your **AM**, **PM**, and **weekly** routines (if your provider assigned them). You can open **My Routine** from here for full steps.
- **Today**: Whether you’ve completed today’s routine and a quick way to log completions.
- **Treatment plans**: Shortcut to your plans and any PDFs from your provider.
- **Tips**: Reminders (e.g. turn on notifications, follow the plan for 7 days before changing anything).

Use the dashboard to see your progress at a glance and jump to **My Routine** or **Treatment Plans**.

---

## Guide

**Guide** is your “how to use SkinAura PRO” page. It’s non-technical and explains:

- **What is SkinAura PRO?** — Your skincare companion: follow your routine, get reminders, track progress with photos, get feedback from your provider.
- **Get started in 3 minutes**  
  1. Turn on notifications (so you get AM/PM reminders).
  2. Review your routine (AM, PM, weekly) and read your provider’s notes.
  3. Take your first photos (baseline, same lighting/angle; clean face, no makeup).
- **3 simple rules for success**  
  1. Follow the plan for 7 days before changing anything.
  2. Don’t add new products without asking your provider.
  3. If something burns or irritates, stop and message your provider.
- **What to expect** — Realistic timeline (e.g. weeks 1–3 stabilization, 4–8 texture/tone, 8–12 clearer trends).
- **Common questions** — e.g. photos, missing days, using other products, irritation, reminders.
- **Need help?** — A **Message your provider** button that takes you to **Notifications** (messages/chat).

Use the Guide whenever you’re unsure how to use the app or what your provider expects.

## My Routine

**My Routine** shows the routines your provider assigned (e.g. Morning, Evening, Weekly).

- For each routine you see **steps** in order (e.g. cleanse, treat, moisturize). Each step may list recommended **products** (name, brand, sometimes a link to buy elsewhere). Your provider’s notes and tips appear here.
- **Mark steps complete**: As you do each step, mark it done for that day. You can often mark an entire routine (e.g. “AM”) as complete in one go.
- **Streak**: Completing routines builds your **streak** and **points**, which show on your Dashboard and in **Achievements**.

If you don’t see a routine, your provider hasn’t assigned one yet—message them from **Notifications** if you think something’s missing.

---

## My Products

**My Products** is your personal product list. Your provider may add products here as part of your routine or treatment plan. You can also add products yourself (e.g. name, brand, notes) to track what you’re using. This is for **tracking and recommendations**, not for purchasing inside the app—you buy products elsewhere (e.g. your provider’s store or retail).

## Face Analysis

**Face Analysis** lets you use on-screen tools to analyze your skin (e.g. face-age or concern areas). The app may give general **tips** (e.g. for fine lines, pores, pigmentation). Results can be shared with your provider; they may appear under **Facial Scan** on their side. This is for education and tracking—it does not replace your provider’s advice.

---

## Treatment Plans

**Treatment Plans** show the plans your provider created for you.

- You see **goals**, **milestones**, **appointments**, and **attached routines and products**.
- **Milestones**: You can mark milestones complete when you’ve reached them (e.g. “Week 4 check-in done”).
- **Appointments**: You can see and update status (e.g. confirm or mark done) if your provider allows.
- **PDFs**: Your provider can attach handouts, care guides, or diagrams. Open or download them from here.

Use this section to stay aligned with your provider’s overall plan and to access their documents.

---

## Notifications

**Notifications** is your inbox inside the app.

- **Alerts**: Reminders (e.g. routine reminders), messages from your provider, and other in-app notices.
- **Invitations**: If a new professional invites you to connect, the invitation appears here. You can **Accept & Connect** or **Decline**. Accepting links you to that provider so you can see their routines and plans.
- **Messages**: You can send and receive messages with your provider here (no need for a separate email for quick questions).

Check Notifications regularly so you don’t miss reminders or provider feedback.

---

## Achievements

**Achievements** shows your **level** (Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Diamond), **points**, and **badges**.

- **Points**: Earned by completing routines and other actions. More points move you up in level.
- **Badges**: Examples include “First Step” (first routine completed), “Week Warrior” (7-day streak), “Consistency Queen” (14-day streak), “Skincare Devotee” (30-day streak). Unlocked badges appear with the date earned; locked ones show what you need to do next.

This is meant to keep you motivated and consistent—it’s not a medical or treatment recommendation.

---

## Leaderboard

**Leaderboard** shows how you compare to other clients of the same provider (or group) by **points** or **streak**. It’s optional fun and encouragement; your provider can decide if it’s enabled.

---

## Help & FAQ

**Help & FAQ** in the client app answers common questions (e.g. how to turn on notifications, what to do if you miss days, when to message your provider). Use it together with the **Guide** for a smooth experience.
